Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's Disease is a neurodegenerative disease, responsible for most of dementia cases. The most commonly early occuring symptom is forgeting recent events. Furthur leading to have problem with language, disorientation, mood swings, loss of motivation, self-neglet and behavioral issues. As it advances the person often withdraws from family and society, wanting to be alone. Gradually, bodily functions are lost, leading to death. Wikipedia

Treatment ?

As the disease is a genetic reasons, there is no current cure for Alzheimer's disease. But the medical treatments given for people with it is mainly to slow the clinical decline, make them feel better, reducing the symptoms, supporting their daily functions. There are some drugs like donepezil, galantamine, and rivastigmine to help maintain memory and thinking skills for early stages. Some antidepressants to help them with depression, mood stabilizers etc. Also cognitive stimulation therapy, memory rehabilitation, and social engagement help preserve quality of life. These all medical treatments are for early stages of Alzheimer, for futher patients with higher stages, we can only give them a good rest for their life.

Statistics

Someone in the world develops dementia every 3 seconds. There are over 55 million people worldwide living with dementia in 2020. This number will almost double every 20 years, reaching 78 million in 2030 and 139 million in 2050. Much of the increase will be in developing countries. Already 60% of people with dementia live in low and middle income countries, but by 2050 this will rise to 71%. The fastest growth in the elderly population is taking place in China, India, and their south Asian and western Pacific neighbours.

Diagnosis

Research shows that most people currently living with dementia have not received a formal diagnosis. In high income countries, only 20-50% of dementia cases are recognised and documented in primary care. This ‘treatment gap’ is certainly much greater in low and middle income countries, with one study in India suggesting 90% remain undiagnosed. If these statistics are extrapolated to other countries worldwide, it suggests that approximately three quarters of people with dementia have not received a diagnosis, and therefore do not have access to treatment, care and organised support that getting a formal diagnosis can provide.Alzheimer's Disease International

Where does this image model come in ?

This image model is capable of classifing a MRI imagery into Alzheimer's disease positive, cognitively normal, Early mild cognitive impairment and Late mild cognitive impairment. Which would be cruially helpful for the diagnosis of Alzheimer's disease for early precautive measures and treatments to abstruct the proceeding of the disease to next stages. This image model is build on CNN architecture with keras and python. Achieveing maximum accuracy in classifing the MRI scans. The data was collected from kaggle, a vast library of dataset. Cleaned, preprocessed and trained on it. The project code can be accessed through the following github link - Alzheimer's-disease.
